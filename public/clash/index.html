Color Clash

Match colours, play action cards on your friends, and empty your hand first.

or join friends

How to play
  1. Everyone gets 7 cards. On your turn, play a card that matches the top card's colour or number/symbol.
  2. Can't play? Draw a card. If it fits you may play it straight away, or pass.
  3. Skip skips the next player, Reverse flips the direction, +2 makes the next player draw two and miss a turn.
  4. Wild lets you pick the colour. Wild +4 does too, and the next player draws four.
  5. Down to your last card? Tap Last card! — if someone catches you first, you draw two.
