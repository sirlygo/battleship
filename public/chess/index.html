How to play
  1. Host a room and share the code; your friend joins with it.
  2. Colours are random, then swap every rematch. White moves first.
  3. Tap a piece, then a highlighted square — or drag it.
  4. All the rules are enforced: castling, en passant, promotion, check, and every kind of draw.
  5. The host can add a clock (e.g. 5 minutes each) before the game starts.
