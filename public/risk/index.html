How to play
  1. The 42 territories are dealt out. Place your starting armies, then take turns.
  2. Reinforce: get 1 army per 3 territories (at least 3), plus a bonus for each whole continent you hold. Trade card sets for more.
  3. Attack: tap your territory, then a neighbouring enemy. You roll up to 3 dice, they roll up to 2; highest dice are compared and ties go to the defender. Blitz fights until someone runs out.
  4. Fortify: move armies once between your territories, then end your turn. Conquer at least one territory to earn a card.
  5. Knock everyone else out to win.
