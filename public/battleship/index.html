Naval Command

Battleship

Hunt down the enemy fleet before they find yours.

or join a friend

How to play
  1. One player hosts and shares the 5-letter room code (or the invite link).
  2. The other player enters the code to join.
  3. Both deploy five ships on a 10×10 grid. Click to drop, R or right-click to rotate, or hit Randomize.
  4. Take turns firing into enemy waters, one shot each. The host can allow a bonus shot after a hit, or pick Salvo mode: one shot per ship you have left.
  5. Sink all five enemy ships to win. Then run it back with a rematch.
  6. Anyone who joins a full room watches as a spectator.
