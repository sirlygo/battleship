The Game of Life

Spin the wheel, pick a career, and race to retirement.

or join friends

How to play
  1. Spin 1–10 and drive. Choose college ($100K in loans, but better careers) or start a career right away.
  2. Collect your salary every time you pass a Payday. Red STOP spaces end your move: graduation, marriage, buying a house, night school and retirement.
  3. LIFE tiles hide cash values revealed at the end. Babies bring LIFE tiles too, and each child pays $50K at retirement.
  4. Short on money? The bank lends $50K at a time — repay $60K each before the end. The richest retiree wins.
