Monopoly

Buy streets, build hotels, make deals — and bankrupt your friends.

or join friends

How to play
  1. Roll two dice and move. Collect $200 every time you pass GO. Doubles roll again — three in a row sends you to Jail.
  2. Land on an unowned property to buy it — or pass and it goes up for auction. Land on someone else's and pay rent.
  3. Own a whole colour set to double its rent, then build houses (up to four) and a hotel for huge rents.
  4. Chance and Community Chest cards can help or hurt. Trade with other players to finish your sets.
  5. Short on cash? Mortgage property or sell buildings. Run out completely and you're bankrupt. Last player standing wins.
