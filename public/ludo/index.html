Ludo

Roll, race round the board and bump your friends back home.

or join friends

How to play
  1. Roll a 6 to bring a token out onto your start square.
  2. Move clockwise round the board, then up your coloured lane to the centre. You need the exact roll to get home.
  3. Land on a rival to bump them back to their yard — except on start squares and ★ stars.
  4. Rolling a 6, bumping someone or getting a token home earns another roll. Three 6s in a row ends your turn.
  5. First to get every token home wins.
