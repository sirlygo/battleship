How to play
  1. One suspect, one weapon and one room are hidden in the case file. The other cards are dealt out.
  2. On your turn, roll and move. Tap a glowing square or room. Rooms in the corners have secret passages.
  3. In a room, suggest a suspect and weapon. Going around the table, the first player holding one of those cards shows it to you in secret.
  4. Your notes can fill in automatically (the host can turn this off). When you're sure, make an accusation. A wrong accusation knocks you out, but you still show cards.
